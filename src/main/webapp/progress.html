💡 BMI Reference Categories

  • Underweight: < 18.5
  • Normal: 18.5 – 24.9
  • Overweight: 25.0 – 29.9
  • Obese: 30.0+

BMI formula: weight(kg) / (height(m) × height(m)). Your profile height is used for automatic calculation.

📌 Best Weigh-In Practices

  • Consistent Time: Weigh yourself at the same time of day (preferably in the morning after waking up).
  • Hydration Impact: Water intake and meal times cause temporary daily fluctuations. Focus on 2–4 week trends.
  • Regular Logs: Log your weight at least 1–2 times per week to generate informative progress charts.
